`vep116_agreement.csv` and `vep116_hg002_columns.csv` are copies, which `manuscript/build_data.py` makes, of the files that `validation/measure_vep116.py` wrote into `benchmarks/results/v0.5.0/`.
Ensembl VEP 116.3 (Docker image `ensemblorg/ensembl-vep@sha256:2a46b0ba4ac544a87136aad92af92050ccbbf09098c1ce306d7e88ef7ed19aa3`, which reports `ensembl-vep 116.3`) ran on 7 November 2026 on the machine of the speed measurements, against the binary built from the tag `v0.5.0-rc.1` (SHA-256 `0063e01bd143...`), and the script records what it ran in `vep116_run.tsv`.
Each count is what `validation/compare_tab.py` reports for the two tab files, and the script assigns every cell of the HG002 sample that differs to one of the causes of `docs/VEP_DIVERGENCE.md`: the `unclassified` column of `vep116_hg002_columns.csv` is 0 in every row, and `generate_figures.py` refuses to draw a figure where it is not.
The counts of the rows that `docs/VEP_DIVERGENCE.md` already had are those that the build at commit 5048fc5 gave on 27 October 2026, to the cell; the 173 variants of the VEP example set, the 400 in-frame deletions and RefSeq's GFF3 are rows that the document did not have, or had only against release 115.1.
The RefSeq row is not drawn in Figure 3, because two of its columns differ by design (`CANONICAL` and `BIOTYPE`: a RefSeq GFF3 carries neither, and fastVEP reads a transcript as VEP's own cache holds it); its differences by column are in `benchmarks/results/v0.5.0/vep116_refseq_columns.csv`.
The same binary and the build at 5048fc5 write the same bytes on the inputs that can be compared without VEP (`validation/compare_builds.py`, `benchmarks/results/v0.5.0/identity_vs_5048fc5/identity.tsv`).
The comparisons with VEP's own Ensembl and RefSeq caches are not in these files: they were made on 27 October 2026 and not repeated, because their extracted caches are no longer on disk.
`manuscript/verify_numbers.py` checks that every number of the two files is in `docs/VEP_DIVERGENCE.md`.
To measure them again, run `validation/measure_vep116.py --bin target/release/fastvep --out benchmarks/results/v0.5.0 [--refseq-gff3 GCF_000001405.40_GRCh38.p14_genomic.gff.gz]` with Docker (about an hour).
