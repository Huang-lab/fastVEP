# The runs of the family v38 against the runs of the family v37

This file sets the classifier of release 0.5.0 (v38), the latest, against v37, the classifier at the close of the review rounds, which the ACMG manuscript described until it was re-based on v38, run for run, on the same inputs and by the same harness.
It is written by `analysis/acmg_benchmark/scripts/20_compare_families.py` from the scored files of the runs and the tables of `manuscript/acmg/data/`, and every number in it is read from them.
ClinVar's 2-star+ classifications are a reference and not ground truth, and every agreement here is with them and nothing stronger.

## Summary

- On the main run, v38 agrees with ClinVar's class for 172 more of the 673,660 records and with its direction for 1,647 more (79.07% to 79.32%); it calls 1,849 more ClinVar benign-side variants benign-side (recall 65.74% to 66.39%) and 4 fewer pathogenic-side variants pathogenic-side (recall 62.48% to 62.47%) (Section 2).
- Opposite-direction calls go from 52 to 51 (27 reference-discordant benign calls and 25 reference-discordant pathogenic calls, then 27 and 24): 1 call leaves the set and 0 enter it (Section 3).
- Across the 104 other pairs with a discrepancy file on both sides, 85 have fewer opposite-direction calls under v38, 13 the same number and 6 more, and over all the pairs 25 variants leave and 30 enter in at least one; the pairs with more are `v37_src_noclinvar_all` (78 to 79), `v37_bs2off` (52 to 72), `v37_bs2prev_3e-5` (52 to 57), `v37_bs2prev_1e-4` (52 to 54), `v37_bs2ac_20` (52 to 59), `v37_bs2ac_100` (52 to 53) (Section 3).
- Of the 12 sweeps, 5 differ in the sign of a step, the position of the knee or the order of their settings, and 1 differs in the knee; the sweeps of the BS2 carrier bar and floor stop being flat and the PM2 sweep under the 2015 table loses most of its range (Section 4).
- Of the 245 effects of the single switches, source removals, hook runs and gap runs on seven measures, 11 changed sign, appeared or vanished, or changed by more than 20%, and 5 of them are of fewer than 10 calls; the one that is large is the removal of the ClinGen file (Section 5).
- In the main run 1 criterion moves its firing count by more than 5% (BS2); PM2 at Supporting strength moves by +2.2% (Section 7).
- On the October release agreement in direction goes from 78.1% to 78.3% and opposite-direction calls from 69 to 68; on the 116,078 new variants agreement goes from 72.3% to 72.6% with 20 and 20 opposite-direction calls, and the rate of opposite-direction calls is 2.2 and 2.3 times the April rate (Section 8).
- The controls reproduce the main run under both classifiers (Section 6).
- 200 of the 541 checks of `verify_numbers.py` give another value under v37 than the manuscript's (527 of 2159 values), 12 claims differ in truth or wording, 5 of 8 marked tables, 18 of 26 sheets of the workbook and 7 of 9 figures differ (Section 9).

## 1. What is compared

The family v37 is the 89 runs on the April set and the 7 runs on the October release that `11_manuscript_runs.py` defines, made on 8 November 2026 by binaries built from commit 5d8004a (`binaries.tsv`).
The family v38 is the same 96 runs made by the classifier of release 0.5.0, the tag `v0.5.0-rc.1`, with the same databases.
18 of them are runs that Section 3.8 also defines under the same names (the sweeps `v38_sw_pm2ad_*`, `v38_sw_pm2ar_*` and `v38_sw_bs2hom_*`, whose grids are the family's), and the harness makes each of them once.
The 96 were made on 8 November 2026 (New York; the provenance records UTC, 2026-11-08) by binaries built from the tag, 7 of them on the October release, several at a time on the ten-core machine (the annotation of one run took 49 to 186 seconds, median 90).
The binaries were `fastvep_v38` (SHA-256 `70b7c684...`, 78 runs), `fastvep_cut38` (`de44db94...`, 9 runs, the point cutoffs) and `fastvep_hook38` (`9886d1b5...`, 9 runs, BP1's own-record count and BP7's SpliceAI threshold), each built from a clean export of the tag in a target directory of its own by `build_manuscript_binaries.sh`, and `binaries_v38.tsv` records them.
The two families are generated by the same functions of `11_manuscript_runs.py` with the name prefix and the binaries as the only parameters, and 96 of the 96 pairs have the same knob, value, default, removed and replaced annotation files and environment in `run_matrix.tsv` and `run_matrix_v38.tsv`, so a pair differs in the classifier and in nothing else.
8 earlier runs of the BS2 carrier settings (`output_v37_bs2off`, `output_v37_bs2prev_*` and `output_v37_bs2ac_*`, made on 8 November 2026 by the binary of v37, like the paper's runs) are paired with the runs of Section 3.8 that have the same grids (`v38_sw_bs2ad_*` and `v38_sw_bs2ac_*`), which makes 104 pairs besides the two baselines in the CSV files; the table below says how many pairs each group holds.

| Group | Pairs |
|:---|---:|
| cutoff | 9 |
| earlier BS2 runs | 8 |
| gap | 3 |
| holdout | 7 |
| hook | 7 |
| interaction | 4 |
| source | 9 |
| sweep | 41 |
| switch | 16 |

The main run `v37` is paired with `v38`, and the run of `14_temporal_holdout.py` on the October release (`v37_oct2026`) with `v38_oct_shipped`.
Everything is over all 673,660 records of the April set and all 781,135 of the October release, as the tables of the paper are.
The 382 ClinVar reference-agreement records of the April set (385 in the October subset) have `.` as their alternative allele and get no call under either classifier, but the binaries of v37 write a record for them and the binaries of v38 do not, so the matrices of v38 hold 673,278 records and the scoring script counts the other 382 apart.
The matrices of v38 here are filled with those records as no-calls, as `13_manuscript_stats.py` does for its twin tables (`fill_nocall`), so that both families have the same denominators; the benign-side recall of v38 is therefore over 284,550 variants, as v37's is, and not over 284,168, as in the tables of Section 3.8.
Three kinds of statement are used below.

- A change is a difference of two counts of the same pair, v38 less v37, and a relative change is that difference over the count under v37.
- The effect of an ablation is the run less the default under the same classifier; it changed sign if the two effects have opposite signs, and by more than 20% if the effect under v38 differs from the one under v37 by more than a fifth of the latter; an effect of fewer than 10 calls under both is called small, because 20% of it is less than two calls.
- The knee of a sweep is where the exchange rate falls the most from one step to the next (Section 2.5 of the manuscript: from hundreds of calls per added error to about a hundred or fewer), taken here as a fall by a factor of 3 or more from a step worth 150 calls per error or more; under both classifiers the rule finds the two knees that the manuscript names (BS1 at 1e-3 and BP7's far boundary at 300, under both versions), none in the sweeps that the manuscript says have none (BS2's homozygote bar, BP7's PhyloP bar and the veto count of BP1), and two that it does not state in these terms (the Likely pathogenic cutoff at 5 points, and the PM2 threshold under the 2015 table at 1e-5, a point inside the span from strict absence to 4e-5 whose rise the manuscript says that v10 took for a knee).

Every number in this file is read from the scored files of the runs and from the twin tables of `manuscript/acmg/data/`, and `20_compare_families.py` writes this file and the five CSV files beside it.

- `v38_family_vs_v37.csv`: one row for every pair, number and cell (the 30 cells of the concordance matrix, 16 headline metrics and the firing count of every criterion), under both families, with the difference and the relative difference.
- `v38_family_vs_v37_sweeps.csv`: every sweep as a curve, one row for every setting.
- `v38_family_vs_v37_effects.csv`: every run of the April set as an effect against the default, under both families, with the flags of the second bullet above.
- `v38_family_vs_v37_opposite.csv`: every opposite-direction call that one run of a pair has and the other lacks.

## 3. The opposite-direction calls

The main run has 52 opposite-direction calls under v37 (27 reference-discordant benign calls and 25 reference-discordant pathogenic calls) and 51 under v38 (27 and 24); 1 call left the set and 0 entered it.
The call that left is RAD21 8:116866762 C>T (splice_acceptor_variant, ENST00000297338.7:c.-32-1G>A), which ClinVar classifies as Likely benign at 2 stars.
v37 called it Likely pathogenic on PVS1, the commit before the change gave the same call on the same criteria, and v38 calls it VUS on PVS1, BS2.
The established ClinGen curations of the gene carry the tag AD, and gnomAD holds the variant in 2,630 of 1,381,576 alleles with 3 homozygotes (population-maximum frequency 0.23%).
v38 adds BS2 beside PVS1, and the combining rule of Section 2.1 of the manuscript (a call on one side stands unless the other side holds evidence of Strong strength or above without reaching a call, and the variant is then a VUS) makes the variant a VUS; in round 6 the reviewer supported ClinVar's call (by a class that the reviewer stated).
No call entered the set: the opposite-direction calls of v38 are a subset of those of v37, and 48 of the 51 are among the 64 rows of `round6_rulings.tsv`, the calls that the reviewer ruled on in round 6.
On the October release the 2-star+ variants have 69 opposite-direction calls under v37 and 68 under v38 (30 and 39, 30 and 38); the same variant leaves it and none enters.
